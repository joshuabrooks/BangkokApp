💡 Money Tips
  • 🚗 Grab app — always cheaper & safer than street taxis
  • 📱 Prepaid SIM at airport — R300–R400 for a week of data
  • 🍜 Street food meals cost R50–R150 — authentic and delicious
  • ⏰ Arrive before 9 AM at major sites to skip queues
  • 🏷️ Haggle at markets — expect 20–30% off asking price
  • 🏨 Hotel breakfast included — take advantage every day
  • 💵 Withdraw R5,000–10,000 at a time from ATMs (lower fees)
🔧 Travel Essentials
🚗 Getting Around

Grab app — download before you leave home. Safer and cheaper than street taxis. Average trip: R60–R150 depending on distance.

🗺️ Offline Maps

Download Bangkok offline maps in Google Maps before boarding. Open app → search Bangkok → Download. Works with zero signal.

💵 Currency & Cash

Thai Baht (THB). 1 USD ≈ R33–35. Cards OK at hotels/restaurants but street vendors are cash only. Withdraw R5,000–10,000 at a time.

🌦️ Weather & Packing

July = rainy season. Short afternoon downpours. Pack light ponchos (R20 locally). Peak heat 36–38°C — finish outdoor sights before noon. SPF 50+!

🗣️ Useful Thai Phrases

👋 Sawasdee krap/ka — Hello
🙏 Kop khun krap/ka — Thank you
🌶️ Mai phet — Not spicy
💰 Tao rai? — How much?
🚿 Hong nam yoo tee nai? — Where is the bathroom?
